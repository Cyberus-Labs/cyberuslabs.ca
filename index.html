About Cyberus Labs

Engineering without artificial boundaries.

Cyberus Labs is a software and technology company focused on turning ideas and real-world problems into practical working products.

The company works across software, automation, integrations, product development, and where appropriate hardware or physical systems.

Cyberus Labs is led by co-founders Derek Proud and Danny Bouchard, combining business, product, customer, and technical execution into one company.
