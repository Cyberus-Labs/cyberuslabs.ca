What is Prometheus?

An end-to-end QA toolkit

Cyberus Labs is built around a core belief: that humans are central to the QA testing experience. Our goal is to augment QA teams with powerful tools, helpful AI and streamlined workflows at the moments that accelerate them the most. Meet Prometheus.

MAIN FEATURES

Constantly Recording - We use OBS (inbuilt or your existing setup) to constantly record what is playing. As soon as a bug is found, the QA engineer presses "Add Bug" and the last x minutes of footage is saved and added to the issue.
